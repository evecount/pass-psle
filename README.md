# pass-psle.sg 🇸🇬

> **Clear the PSLE haze. Without the mental breakdown.**  
> Dedicated to civic good and the education of Singapore children.  
> **In loving memory of Mdm Su Kim Chu**, the first principal of Haig Girls' School.

---

### Civic Mission & Philosophy

PSLE should never break a child. Education is meant to lift children up, build self-worth, and spark curiosity—not reduce 12-year-olds to an AL aggregate score under crippling stress.

`pass-psle.sg` is built as an open, free public service:
1. **100% Free & Open**: Zero commercial paywalls, zero ads, zero token limits.
2. **Provenance-Backed**: Direct questions and heuristics modeled on official SEAB/MOE syllabi, Yayasan Mendaki revision series, and Singapore top primary school prelims.
3. **Deterministic Solutions**: Every question features pre-baked, crystal-clear breakdowns explaining **why the right answer is right** and **why common misconceptions are wrong**.
4. **Student Decompression Anchor**: The "Take a Breath" emergency protocol and tutor SOS lifeline.

---

### The Dedication: Mdm Su Kim Chu
Mdm Su Kim Chu served as the pioneering first principal of **Haig Girls' School** (founded in 1951), championing character development, quiet dignity, resilience, and compassion in educating generations of young girls in Singapore. This project carries forward that timeless ethos: every child matters, and education must protect their humanity first.

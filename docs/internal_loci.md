### **Q: Are there any specific target customers you have identified, that you hope to reach during our program? (Finnish Context)**

**A:** Yes, in collaboration with our European partner, maria.io, we have identified three strategic customer profiles in Finland that we aim to engage during the program:

1.  **The Anchor Enterprise Client (Nokia):** As a global technology leader with deep Finnish roots, Nokia represents the ideal enterprise partner. Their extensive internal training requirements for engineering, compliance, and sales teams make them a perfect fit for a pilot of our white-label AI platform. Securing a pilot with Nokia would provide immense technical validation and a clear path to scaling within the European technology sector.

2.  **The Research & Innovation Partner (Aalto University):** We will leverage the accelerator's network to formalize a research pilot with a leading department at Aalto University, such as the School of Science. We will offer our platform for free to create interactive learning modules for their AI or computer science courses. In return, we gain permission to use anonymized learning data for a joint research paper, establishing academic credibility in the EU and creating a powerful case study.

3.  **The Public Sector & Social Impact Partner (The City of Helsinki):** Helsinki is a world leader in digital public services and education. We will propose a pro-bono pilot with a specific department, such as the Education Division, to transform their public outreach or internal training materials into an accessible, multilingual learning tool. This demonstrates our commitment to social impact and showcases our platform's value for the public sector, a key market in the Nordic region.

This three-tiered approach allows us to pursue a high-value enterprise lead, solidify our technology through a strategic research partnership, and build powerful social proof through a meaningful public sector engagement.

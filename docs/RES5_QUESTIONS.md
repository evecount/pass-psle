Chapter 1: FAA & FAR – Financial Advisers and Representatives (20 Questions)
Question 1. A foreign company established in Singapore provides financial advisory services but relies on its approved related corporation (licensed in Singapore) for oversight, operational systems, and compliance. Under what condition is this foreign company EXEMPTED from holding a Financial Adviser’s Licence under the FAA?
A. The arrangement must be formally approved by the MAS.
B. The foreign company must limit its clients strictly to Institutional Investors.
C. The financial advisory services provided must be related only to Capital Markets Products.
D. The foreign company must pay a fixed annual fee in lieu of holding a licence.
Correct Answer: A
Rationale: Regulation 32CB of the FAR provides an exemption for foreign companies operating under an approved arrangement with a related licensed corporation, provided the arrangement has been formally approved by the MAS (Study Text Ch 1, 4.8(k)).

Question 2. Which of the following corporate entities is explicitly listed in Section 20(1) of the FAA as an Exempt Financial Adviser (EFA) and thus not required to hold a separate FA Licence?
A. An Approved Trustee under the SFA.
B. A public accountant whose advisory business is solely incidental to his accounting practice.
C. A company or co-operative society licensed under the Insurance Act 1996.
D. A foreign company operating under an approved cross-border arrangement.
Correct Answer: C
Rationale: The FAA Section 20(1) lists categories of existing financial institutions that are automatically exempt from holding a separate FA license (Study Text Ch 1, 4.7(c)). Options A and B are Excluded Financial Advisers under the First Schedule.

Question 3. A Provisional Representative (PR) is an individual who meets entry requirements but has not yet passed the relevant CMFAS exams. What is the ** primary objective** of introducing the Provisional Representative scheme?

C. To permit individuals to work for more than one related corporation during the initial phase.
D. To provide a mentorship period before mandatory compliance with the Balanced Scorecard Framework.
Correct Answer: B
Rationale: The PR scheme's objective is specifically "to facilitate the relocation of experienced individuals currently licensed in an overseas jurisdiction" (Study Text Ch 1, 12.1).

Question 4. If an Appointed Representative ceases to provide financial advisory services on 15 March, by what date at the latest must their principal notify the MAS of this cessation?

B. 16 March (the next business day).
C. 29 March (within 14 days).

Rationale: The principal must provide particulars of cessation no later than the next business day after the cessation occurs (Study Text Ch 1, 11.6, citing FAA S23(8) and FAR Reg 12A).

Question 5. A client is classified as an Accredited Investor (AI) primarily based on their financial assets. What is the minimum value required for financial assets (net of any related liabilities) for an individual to qualify as an AI?
A. S$2 million (net personal assets).
B. S$1 million (net financial assets).
C. S$300,000 (income in the preceding 12 months).
D. S$10 million (corporation net assets).
Correct Answer: B
Rationale: An individual qualifies as an AI if their "financial assets (net of any related liabilities) exceed in value S$1 million" (Study Text Ch 1, 6.8(I)(a)(ii)). S$2 million is for net personal assets.

Question 6. Which of the following is TRUE regarding the mandatory contents of the Public Register of Representatives maintained by the MAS?
A. The disciplinary action history taken by MAS is recorded, but only for the past 5 years.
B. The representative's date of birth is included to verify the minimum age criterion.
C. The names of every past principal must be recorded on the register.
D. The register includes the representative's full residential address.
Correct Answer: C
Rationale: The Public Register must record the representative's name, the name of their current principal, and every past principal (Study Text Ch 1, 9.8(b)). The Register does not disclose the date of birth (B) or the full residential address (D) publicly, though this information is held by MAS internally. Disciplinary history retention is not limited to 5 years (A).

Question 7. A Provisional Representative (PR) is prohibited from acting in that capacity for how long, unless transferred to an Appointed Representative status?

Question 8. The principle of Accountability under the FAA and FAR is captured primarily by which rule?

Question 9. What is the maximum fine for a representative who contravenes the rule that they shall not, at any one time, be a representative of more than one principal (unless the principals are related corporations)?

Question 10. Under the criteria for refusal to grant a financial adviser's licence (FAA S8(4)), the MAS may refuse an application without giving the applicant an opportunity to be heard if:

Question 12. A Licensed Financial Adviser (LFA) applies to MAS for a licence renewal. How often is the LFA required to renew its licence?

Question 13. Which of the following activities falls within the definition of "financial advisory service" under the FAA?

Question 14. An individual ceases to be an Appointed Representative under which of the following circumstances?

Rationale: Section 23(4) of the FAA states the cessation circumstances, including when the individual's principal ceases to provide that type of financial advisory service (Study Text Ch 1, 11.3(b)). Temporary career breaks (A) or change of address (C) do not automatically cause cessation.

Question 15. Which of the following is not included in the definition of an "investment product" under the FAA?

Question 18. The concept of Consistency as a principle underpinning the FAA and FAR applies in which of the following manners?

Question 20. What is the maximum percentage of an LFA's net asset value that may be applied as a deductible in its Professional Indemnity Insurance (PII) policy?

Chapter 2: FAA & FAR – Conduct of Business, Powers of MAS & Offences (15 Questions)
Question 21. A Licensed Financial Adviser (LFA) fails to make a recommendation with a reasonable basis, causing a client to suffer a loss. What is the most immediate consequence of this failure under Section 36(3) of the FAA?

Correct Answer: C

Question 22. When a Licensed Financial Adviser (LFA) is required to establish and maintain a separate bank account for its life insurance broking premiums (Insurance Broking Premium Accounts - S41), which of the following is an ALLOWABLE condition for withdrawal of monies from this account under FAR Regulation 20(3)?

Rationale: FAR Regulation 20(3) lists specific circumstances for withdrawal. Payment to or for an insurer for amounts due is explicitly allowed (Study Text Ch 2, 6.3(b)). Investing is restricted to deposits with licensed banks (Study Text Ch 2, 6.3(c)).

Question 23. Which of the following accurately describes the regulatory scope regarding the negotiation and placement of risk with unlicensed insurers by an LFA under Section 42 of the FAA?

Question 24. An LFA's director wilfully omits to make an entry in a record concerning the business affairs of the LFA. This constitutes an offence under Section 113 (Falsification of Records). Question 25. Under Section 68 of the FAA, the MAS may issue a Prohibition Order (PO) that can prohibit a person from:

Question 30. What action is a Licensed Financial Adviser (LFA) required to take if the realization of an investment made from its Insurance Broking Premium Accounts results in the amount received being less than the amount initially invested?

This ensures the premium account integrity is maintained.

Chapter 3A: MAS Notice FAA-N16 (Recommendations, Suitability, CKA/CAR) (15 Questions)
Question 36. When assessing a client's investment experience for the purpose of the Customer Knowledge Assessment (CKA) for an Unlisted Specified Investment Product (SIP), which of the following transactions is the FA required to consider?

Correct Answer: B
Rationale: Annex 2 of FAA-N16, criterion 1(c)(ii), explicitly defines the relevant transaction history for unlisted SIPs as: "client has transacted in any Specified Investment Products which are neither listed nor quoted... at least 6 times in the preceding 3 years" (Study Text Ch 3A). Listed SIPs (C) are assessed under CAR.

Question 37. A representative makes a recommendation to a client regarding an investment product. Which of the following information is the FA NOT typically required to document as part of the "reasonable efforts" of documentation (FAA-N16, Par 35)?
A. The client's statement of his investment objectives.
B. The FA's assessment of the disadvantages of the investment product based on the client's circumstances.
C. The FA's reasonable basis for making the recommendation to the client.
D. A full list of the client's non-investment assets, such as personal vehicles.
Correct Answer: D
Rationale: Paragraph 35 specifically mandates documenting (a) client objectives, (b) the FA's reasonable basis, and (c) the FA's assessment of disadvantages. While non-investment assets (D) are part of the fact-find (Par 11), they are not listed as an explicit component of the documentation of the basis for the recommendation under Paragraph 35 (Study Text Ch 3A).

Question 38. Which of the following transactions, if executed by a client who previously had a positive Customer Account Review (CAR) outcome, will NOT cause the CAR outcome's validity to be extended beyond the initial 3-year period?

Correct Answer: D
Rationale: The validity period of the CAR outcome is extended only if the client transacts in a Listed Specified Investment Product (SIP) more than once during the 3-year period (FAA-N16, Par 27T). A listed corporate bond (D) is typically an Excluded Investment Product (EIP), and therefore does not count toward extending the CAR validity.

Question 39. A financial adviser (FA) is making a recommendation on an Overseas-Listed Investment Product for the first time to a natural person client on 1 June 2025. What is the FA's immediate requirement?

Rationale: Paragraphs 41C and 41D of FAA-N16 mandate that the FA must provide the Annex 4 Risk Warning Statement and obtain the client's acknowledgement in written form (or otherwise) before making the recommendation for the first time on or after 8 October 2018 (Study Text Ch 3A).

Question 40. A financial adviser (FA) may rely on information previously provided by a client for the "Know Your Client" requirements (FAA-N16, Par 11) only if:

Question 148. When interpreting a client's financial position using personal ratios (Chapter 23), a consistently high Debt-to-Asset Ratio (e.g., 70%) suggests which critical financial status point?
A. The client is highly liquid, as assets exceed liabilities significantly.

C. The client's Debt Service Ratio is dangerously low (below 35%).
D. The client is not saving at the recommended 10% of their annual income.
Correct Answer: B
Rationale: Debt-to-Asset Ratio = Liabilities / Assets. If the ratio is 70% (0.7), it means 70% of assets are financed by debt. Since Net Worth = Assets - Liabilities, the Net Worth is only 30% of the Total Assets, indicating high leverage and low solvency (Study Text Ch 23, 4.6).

Question 150. The Balance Sheet (Statement of Net Worth) and the Statement of Cash Flows differ primarily because the Statement of Cash Flows records information:

**Metric:** Precision Focus Ratio.

### 2. The "Skilled Technician" Profile (The Troubleshooter)

**Variable 5 (Correction Pulse):** Near-zero backspacing. The technician does the "math" in their head first. The input is a "one-strike" execution.

**Variable 7 (Stamina Decay):** A flat slope. Even at hour 10, the accuracy and latency remain identical to minute 1. This measures "Systemic Robustness."

**Metric:** Execution Efficiency.

### 3. The "Innovator" Profile (The Cross-Domain Synthesizer)

**Variable 4 (Semantic KPC Variance):** High Variance. An innovator solves a problem in one domain using the vocabulary of another (e.g., solving biology problems with engineering terms).

**Variable 6 (Topic Gravity):** Their "bookmarks" are always at the intersections of two unrelated topics. They don't dwell in the center of a field; they dwell at the edges.

**Metric:** Synthetical Leap Index.

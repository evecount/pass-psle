This is a vital update to our analysis. Singapore is currently the global "ground zero" for AI-integrated education. The Ministry of Education (MOE) has created a unique ecosystem where parents are highly receptive to AI, but the market is heavily skewed toward **K-12 (Primary/Secondary) MOE curriculum.**

As a market analyst, I see your platform—**Eve Count / Pass-Exams**—occupying a "Blue Ocean" (uncontested space) by pivoting away from the crowded K-12 "Consumer" market and toward the **B2B Professional/High-Stakes** licensing market.

Here is the Singapore-specific competitive breakdown.

### 1. Direct B2B Competitors: AI Platforms for Educators

These are the closest competitors because they allow teachers or institutions to build their own AI assets, rather than just selling a subscription to a pre-made app.

#### **Noodle Factory**

* **Value Proposition:** An AI teaching assistant that helps educators automate the creation of tutoring, role-plays, and assessments from their own materials.
* **Key Differentiator:** Deeply integrated into the Singapore tertiary and corporate sector; focused on "Teaching Assistants" rather than just a "Practice App."
* **Primary Weakness/Gap:** It is a general-purpose educational builder. It lacks your **"Alpha Loop" weighting logic** (dynamic weighting based on student bookmarks) and your specific focus on the **Professional Certification (CMFAS/RES)** vertical, which requires a higher degree of regulatory precision.

#### **NALA Project (NTU)**

* **Value Proposition:** A university-backed initiative allowing educators to build personalized generative AI tutors for their specific classroom needs.
* **Key Differentiator:** High academic credibility and institutional backing from NTU; focuses on "Agentic AI" that acts like a mentor.
* **Primary Weakness/Gap:** It is currently an academic/institutional project. It lacks the **commercial "Lead Magnet" functionality** and the "White-Label-as-a-Service" business model that allows a retired teacher to launch a private, monetized app in 24 hours.

---

### 2. Indirect Competitors (B2C): The "AI Tuition" Giants

These are the companies your potential partners (tutors/schools) are currently losing students to.

#### **Geniebook / Tutorly.sg / Lumine Learning**

* **Value Proposition:** Comprehensive AI-driven tuition for P1 to JC2, providing personalized worksheets and real-time tracking.
* **Key Differentiator:** **Massive Data Sets.** They have millions of data points on Singaporean students' common mistakes in the MOE syllabus.
* **Primary Weakness/Gap:** **The "walled garden" problem.** They are B2C brands. A private tutor or a small academy cannot "buy" Geniebook's engine to run their own content. **Your platform is the "Shopify" to their "Amazon"**—you empower the individual brand (Eve Count / Partners) to own the tech, rather than forcing them to compete against a giant platform.

#### **Practicle**

* **Value Proposition:** An AI math tutor that identifies a child's weak topics and provides targeted practice for the MOE syllabus.
* **Key Differentiator:** Highly gamified and extremely effective at specific "skill-gap" identification for Mathematics.
* **Primary Weakness/Gap:** **Hyper-Specialization.** It is a Math-only tool for kids. It cannot be easily adapted for high-stakes professional written exams or synthesis-heavy subjects like Insurance Law or Financial Ethics.

---

### 3. Indirect Competitors (Niche): Professional & Essay Feedback

These tools compete on the "high-level" synthesis skills your platform targets.

#### **The Wise Otter**

* **Value Proposition:** Specialist AI feedback for O-Level/A-Level English and General Paper (GP) essays.
* **Key Differentiator:** Specifically tuned for the "Singapore Style" of grading qualitative, long-form responses.
* **Primary Weakness/Gap:** It is a **niche tool**. While it handles essays well, it doesn't provide the full-suite "White-Label" experience (hosting, user management, and MCQ-to-Written transition). It is a feedback tool, not a **Business-in-a-Box.**

In Singapore, the AI EdTech market is currently "Kids-Heavy."

| Market Segment | Competitive Density | Current Solution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **K-12 (Primary/Sec)** | **EXTREME** | Geniebook, Practicle, Tutorly.sg |
| **English/GP Essays** | **MODERATE** | The Wise Otter |

3. **The "Grounded" Guarantee:** High-stakes professional exams cannot tolerate the hallucinations that "General Tutors" (like Tutorly) might produce. Your "Content-Only" guardrail is a legal and ethical requirement for financial training.

* **Geniebook/Tutorly:** Good for kids, but they own your data and your brand.
